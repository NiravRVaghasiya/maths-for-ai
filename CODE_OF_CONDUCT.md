# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a learning project, and its community should be a welcoming place for people at every
level — from someone seeing linear algebra for the first time to a researcher extending the
advanced modules. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps this community:

- Being patient and respectful with learners' questions — there are no "stupid" questions about
  the mathematics here.
- Giving feedback on contributions that is specific, kind, and focused on the work (cite the
  relevant framework doc, suggest a concrete fix).
- Assuming good faith, and correcting mistakes — including mathematical ones — graciously.
- Welcoming contributors from different disciplines: a mathematician, an ML researcher, an
  educator, and a software engineer each bring something the others don't.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Condescension toward learners or dismissiveness about someone's level of background.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any other project communication — and when an individual is representing the project in public
spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer
through their GitHub profile or via a private report. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security
of the reporter of any incident, and may take any action deemed appropriate, from a warning to a
permanent ban from project spaces.
